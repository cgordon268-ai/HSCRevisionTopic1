The PIES needs of service users

Service users have physical, intellectual, emotional and social needs. Many are shared by everyone, but how they show up depends on the service user group.

BP3

Meeting needs in different settings

For each setting, match the need to the way staff could meet it. Then plan a 12-mark answer.

Read the question carefully. You must adapt your answer to the service user and the setting in the question. “Providing meals” is vague; “offering a choice of nutritious meals at protected mealtimes on the ward” is specific.

BP5

Effects of ill-health on individuals and families

Ill-health can affect income, leisure activities, relationships, education and employment, for the person who is ill and for their family. Some effects are positive.

Categorising

Who and what?

For each effect, decide who it affects and which area of life it is about.
